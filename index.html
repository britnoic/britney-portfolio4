Things I've worked on

01🍓

Personal finance · C programming

MyBudgetBuddy

A clearer picture of where the money goes.

MyBudgetBuddy is an expense-tracking program designed to help users record spending, organise purchases by category and review their weekly and monthly habits. It includes profile details, custom expense categories, payment-type tracking and summaries that compare spending with monthly salary.

Expense trackingReportsFile handling

Project focus: turning individual transactions into useful spending summaries.

02🍵

Health & wellness · System analysis

Fitness and Wellness Tracking App

Small habits, progress you can actually see.

This app concept brings fitness and everyday well-being into one place. Users can log workouts, track meals and hydration, set personal goals and follow their progress over time. Reminders and access to fitness professionals can help make healthy routines feel more manageable.

Activity loggingGoal settingProgress tracking

Project focus: supporting healthier routines through organised tracking and feedback.

03🌱

Agriculture · Object-oriented design

DirectFarm Samarahan System

Helping local produce find its way to buyers.

DirectFarm Samarahan explores a more organised way to connect farmers and buyers. The system models the journey from produce listings and orders to packing, delivery and payment-related records, helping make the different roles and steps easier to manage.

OrdersDelivery workflowSystem modelling

Project focus: connecting the people and steps involved in getting produce to customers.

04🦧

Conservation · C programming

Wildlife Tracking and Reporting System

Turning wildlife observations into information worth acting on.

The Wildlife Tracking and Reporting System is designed to help conservation teams keep species records and log sightings with details such as location, date, time and observed behaviour. It can summarise sightings and highlight species that may need more attention based on population status and sighting patterns.

Species recordsSighting logsPriority reports

Project focus: organising field observations so they can support conservation planning.

05🗓️

Room reservations · System analysis

Room Booking System

Less booking confusion, more time for the event itself.

This proposed web-based system addresses the challenges of manually booking shared rooms, including misplaced records and overlapping reservations. Users can check room availability, save booking details and send feedback about room facilities, helping staff manage spaces more clearly.

Availability checkingReservationsUser feedback

Project focus: making shared-room booking and facility reporting easier to follow.
